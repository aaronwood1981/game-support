Privacy policy

Your adventure.
Your privacy.

The app privacy statements below apply specifically to Lights Over Harrow Farm. Different games may have different requirements.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Lights Over Harrow Farm

Lights Over Harrow Farm does not collect personal data.

  • No account is required.
  • No analytics are used.
  • No ads are shown.
  • No in-app purchases are included.
  • No tracking or third-party tracking is used.
  • Stone & Tankard Games does not receive personal data through the app.

Your save stays with you

Save data for Lights Over Harrow Farm stays local on your device. Stone & Tankard Games does not receive, store or access that save data.

Games in development

The location-based kingdom builder is an unreleased project. The no-account and local-only statements above describe Lights Over Harrow Farm, not the kingdom builder.

Game-specific privacy information will be provided before a public release of the kingdom builder, including details about location features, accounts and online services.

Future releases and changes

If future games or updates add advertising, analytics, in-app purchases or online services, this policy will be updated or supplemented with game-specific information explaining the data involved and how it is handled.

Questions about privacy?

Email stoneandtankardgames@gmail.com. If you contact the studio by email, your message and email address are received so the studio can respond. Please do not include passwords or sensitive information.
