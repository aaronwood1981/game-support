Player support

A little help
along the way.

Having trouble with Lights Over Harrow Farm? Send a note directly to the studio. Questions and feedback are welcome, too.

Get in touch

stoneandtankardgames@gmail.com

This is a solo studio, so replies may take a little time. Your message will be read with care.

Help me find the problem

Please include these details when reporting an issue:

Game title
The game you are contacting the studio about.
Device
Your phone or tablet model.
OS version
The operating system and version installed on your device.
App version
The version of the game you are using.
What happened
What you expected, what happened instead, and the steps leading up to it.

A little extra context

A screenshot can help with display issues. For audio, saving or progress problems, mention what you were doing just before the issue occurred. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.
